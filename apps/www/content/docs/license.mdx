## reelcn

Every file in the registry — everything under `src/reelcn/` once you install it — is MIT licensed. Copy it, change it, ship it.

## Remotion

reelcn builds on Remotion, which has its own license. Individuals, non-profits and companies of up to 3 people use it free; larger companies need a Remotion company license.

## Fonts

Theme fonts (Inter, Instrument Serif, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono, Bricolage Grotesque) load from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License, at render time. Nothing is redistributed by reelcn.
