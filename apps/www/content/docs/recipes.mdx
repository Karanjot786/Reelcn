---
title: Recipes
description: Eight prompts that turn a request into a finished video, with the template and scene order each one uses.
---

Install the [agent skill](/docs/agent-skill), then type one of these. The agent picks the template, sets the props, checks a contact sheet and renders.

## Changelog

Prompt: "Write a changelog video for Relay 2.4, released September 2026. New: scheduled releases. Improved: the editor opens twice as fast. Fixed: links in footnotes open correctly."

Builds the [changelog](/docs/components/changelog) template at 16:9, 15 to 30 seconds.

Scenes: title with the version and date, bullets of up to three changes tagged New, Improved or Fixed, a call to action with the changelog URL.

## Data story

Prompt: "Make a data story: 1.28 million videos rendered this year, up from 310,000 last year, with renders per day climbing from 40k in January to 164k in September."

Builds the [data-story](/docs/components/data-story) template at 16:9, 4 seconds per section plus the title.

Scenes: title with the finding, a stat for the single biggest number, then a bar, line or donut chart per comparison.

## Feature short

Prompt: "Make a short for Relay. Hook: stop writing release notes by hand. Feature: Relay drafts them from your merged pull requests. Cta: try Relay free."

Builds the [feature-short](/docs/components/feature-short) template at 9:16, 12 to 20 seconds.

Scenes: hook text, title with the feature, a phone screen recording, call to action.

## Podcast clip

Prompt: "Make a teaser for episode 42 of Frame by Frame. Use the clip where our guest says the best render farm is the one you never have to think about."

Builds the [podcast-teaser](/docs/components/podcast-teaser) template at 9:16, 15 to 45 seconds.

Scenes: the quote as a caption card over a trimmed clip of the episode, with the show name and cover art.

## Product launch

Prompt: "Make a launch video for Relay, our release notes tool. Features: write once, ship on merge, see who read it."

Builds the [product-launch](/docs/components/product-launch) template at 16:9, 25 to 45 seconds.

Scenes: logo, title, one per feature, a browser shot per screenshot, call to action.

## Talking head short

Prompt: "Make a talking-head short from take-3.mp4. Hook: most videos lose you in three seconds. Emphasize the words three and seconds."

Builds the [talking-head-short](/docs/components/talking-head-short) template at 9:16, aim for 20 to 60 seconds.

Scenes: the hook for the first three seconds, then the video with captions and two to four emphasized words.

## Tutorial

Prompt: "Make a tutorial titled Add a storyboard to your project. Step one: run npx shadcn add @reelcn/storyboard. Step two: show the Composition code. Step three: screenshot the studio."

Builds the [tutorial](/docs/components/tutorial) template at 16:9, set by typing time, aim under 90 seconds.

Scenes: title with the outcome, one step per scene as terminal, code or screenshot.

## YouTube intro

Prompt: "Make a channel intro for Northwind Labs. Title: Building a render farm in an afternoon."

Builds the [youtube-intro](/docs/components/youtube-intro) template at 16:9, 4 to 6 seconds, paired with the youtube-outro template at 10 seconds as the end screen.

Scenes: title text reveal over a brand sweep and gradient mesh background.

## Write your own

When no recipe fits, the agent writes a [storyboard](/docs/storyboard) instead: a JSON file with a theme and a list of scenes. Ask for the video and say to use a storyboard.
