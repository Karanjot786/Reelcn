# Sound effect licences

Every sound here is generated by `scripts/sfx-build.ts` with ffmpeg, so reelcn holds the rights and releases them under CC0-1.0. Re-run `pnpm sfx:build` to reproduce the pack byte for byte.

Loudness target: -16 LUFS integrated (ffmpeg `loudnorm`, TP=-1.5 dBTP, LRA=11). Sounds under ~200ms can't reach -16 LUFS integrated — loudnorm's gating window needs more signal than a short hit provides — so those files are instead peak-limited at -1.5 dBTP and land below the -16 LUFS target. This is expected for short sounds, not a bug.
